# Competitor and Pattern Research — October 2026

What comparable IT-support and software firms do on their websites, how mudhotech.com compares, and what was changed as a result.

## How this was done, and its limits

- Web searches only. This environment's network policy blocks direct visits to the sites (and to mudhotech.com itself), so nothing below comes from looking at a competitor's pages in a browser. Findings are what search results and published guides report.
- Directory listings and vendor blogs are marketing material. Their statistics (conversion lifts and similar) are unverified and are not relied on here.
- Before copying anything from a competitor, look at their site yourself.

## Changed in this round

1. **Facts row under the homepage hero:** registered company, office, hours, reply time. Every value is read from `src/data/company.ts` or `src/data/commitments.ts`, so it changes when they do.
2. **IT Support request form:** optional "Device or system" and "How should we get to it?" (bring it to the Graniteside office / on-site visit / not sure). Both are folded into the request message in Django, so the admin inbox shows them with no database migration.
3. **3D network globe behind the homepage headline** (separate request, same branch).

## The biggest remaining wins all need the owner

In order of likely impact for a Zimbabwean buyer:

1. **How clients pay and are invoiced** (OPEN-QUESTIONS #18). Local competitors state this prominently; a business buyer needs fiscal or VAT invoices.
2. **Real proof** (#17, #20): one permitted case study, a founding year, and any certification or partner status.
3. **"From" prices for common jobs** (#19).
4. **Repair status tracking** by reference number: a backend feature, worth building once repair volume justifies it.
